1. CORE GAMEPLAY & HOUSE RULES
================================================================================
1.1. Draw Card Stacking:
     - Allow optional stacking of penalty cards (+2 on +2, +4 on +4, or Dark Draw 5 on Draw 5).
     - When a player cannot counter-stack, they draw the accumulated total (e.g. +8 or +15).
     - Configurable toggle in room settings: "Stacking: ON / OFF".

1.2. Jump-In Rule:
     - If a player holds an EXACT match (identical face, color, and value) to the discard top,
       they can play it out of turn immediately before the next turn resolves.
     - Play order then resumes from that player, skipping everyone in between.

1.3. 7-0 Hand Rotation Rule:
     - Playing a '7' allows the player to choose any opponent and swap entire hands.
     - Playing a '0' causes all players to pass their hands in the current direction of play.
     - Adds high-stakes tactical chaos, especially in 5-color and Dark mode.

1.4. Draw-to-Match vs Draw-One Mode:
     - Standard UNO rules offer two variants:
       a) Draw-One: Player draws 1 card; if playable, they may play it; otherwise turn ends.
       b) Draw-to-Match: Player must keep drawing until a playable card is found.
     - Allow room host to select between Draw-One and Draw-to-Match.

1.5. Wild Draw 4 Challenge Mechanic:
     - When Wild Draw 4 is played, the target player can challenge the move if they suspect
       the player held a matching color card in hand.
     - Server verifies: if guilty, the player who played WD4 draws 4 as penalty;
       if innocent, the challenger draws 6 cards (4 + 2 penalty).

1.6. Dark Side Penalty Escalation:
     - In Two-Side mode, make Dark side action cards progressively harsher if a match stays on
       the Dark side for more than 5 consecutive turns (e.g., Draw 5 becomes Draw 7).

1.7. 2v2 Team Battle Mode:
     - 4 players divided into two opposing teams (North/South vs East/West).
     - Team members cannot see each other's cards, but win jointly when either teammate empties hand.
     - Team scoring combines remaining cards from both opponents.

1.8. Showdown 1v1 Duel Mode:
     - Fast-paced 1v1 with a 7-second turn timer.
     - Sudden death rules: cards count down from a 40-card speed deck.

1.9. Discard All of Color:
     - Introduce the custom action card "Discard All [Color]" allowing a player to discard all
       cards of the designated color simultaneously.

1.10. Flip Shield Card:
     - A rare Dark Side card that protects the holder from the effects of the next FLIP,
       retaining their current hand orientation while other players are flipped.

2. SOCIAL & MULTIPLAYER SYSTEMS
================================================================================
2.1. Instant URL / QR Code Room Join:
     - Generate shareable direct invite URLs: `https://game.domain/join/7X3K9M`.
     - Display a QR code in the lobby so players on mobile can join by pointing camera at screen.

2.2. Persistent Friends System:
     - Add players via unique Friend Code (e.g. `UNO-7829-K4`).
     - Real-time online status indicator (In Lobby, In Match, Idle, Offline).
     - One-click "Invite to Match" and "Join Friend's Room" buttons.

2.3. Clan & Guild System:
     - Players create or join Clans (up to 30 members) with custom clan tags, badges, and banners.
     - Weekly Clan Leaderboards based on cumulative clan match wins.
     - Clan trophies that unlock exclusive clan frames and card backs.

2.4. Spectator Mode:
     - Allow non-playing users to join rooms as spectators.
     - Spectators view public board state with a 5-second broadcast delay to prevent screen-cheating.
     - Spectator emoji cheers and live chat feed.

2.5. In-Game WebRTC Voice Chat:
     - Integrated peer-to-peer spatial voice chat between friends in private rooms.
     - Audio panning reflects player positions around the table (left player hears on left ear).
     - Push-to-talk and one-tap mute options.

2.6. Rematch System:
     - On Match End (ResultScreen), provide a "Rematch" button.
     - When all players accept, restarts match instantly in the same room without re-hosting.

2.7. Match Replay & Highlight Recording:
     - Automatically record match action logs (`move_history`).
     - Players can replay their best matches, step forward/backward, and export animated GIFs.

2.8. Social Share Cards:
     - On victory, generate a stylish, downloadable image card showing match score, win streak,
       and player avatar ready to share on Instagram, WhatsApp, or Discord.

7. SERVER ARCHITECTURE, SCALING & ANTI-CHEAT
================================================================================
7.1. Redis Pub/Sub Horizontal Scaling:
     - Integrate `@socket.io/redis-adapter` so multiple Node.js server instances can run across
       Docker containers or Kubernetes pods, sharing room state seamlessly.

7.2. Session Reconnection & State Recovery:
     - Issue a short-lived cryptographically signed reconnection token (`reconToken`) to each client.
     - If client disconnects due to mobile Wi-Fi drop or browser refresh, client reconnects within
       45 seconds, presents token, and resumes their exact hand and room without losing state.

7.3. Authoritative Action Rate Limiting:
     - Server enforces minimum time delta between client actions (e.g., minimum 250ms between plays)
       to eliminate auto-clicker scripts and automated macro cheating.

7.4. Server-Side Move Validation:
     - Continue absolute server authoritativeness: client never informs server what card is valid;
       client only sends requested `cardId`, and server evaluates against game rules.

7.5. Database Caching Layer:
     - Cache global leaderboard queries in Redis with a 60-second TTL to avoid slamming PostgreSQL
       with repetitive read traffic.

7.6. Room Eviction & Garbage Collection:
     - Automatic teardown of inactive/abandoned rooms after 15 minutes of inactivity to preserve memory.

8.3. Bot Chat & Reaction Simulation:

8.4. Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) Engine:

       draw probabilities and card distributions.

10.1. Colorblind Accessibility Symbols:
      - Add distinct geometric symbols to every card corner alongside colors:
        - Red = Diamond (◆)
        - Blue = Circle (●)
        - Green = Triangle (▲)
        - Yellow = Star (★)
        - Purple = Hexagon (⬡)
      - Ensures 100% playable experience for protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia.

10.2. Multilingual Localization:
      - Full i18n support with JSON translation dictionaries for:
        - English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Arabic.

10.3. Screen Reader (ARIA) Compatibility:
      - Descriptive ARIA live regions announcing moves:
        - "Player 2 played a Blue 7. Active color is Blue."
        - "It is now your turn. 5 cards in your hand."

10.4. Font Size & Contrast Scaling:
      - User preference toggle for large UI text and high-contrast card faces.
